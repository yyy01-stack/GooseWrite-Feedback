# GooseWrite - Official Feedback & Support Tracker

Welcome to the GooseWrite Community! 🚀

GooseWrite is an all-in-one workstation that connects your workflows into a seamless information stream. To protect your privacy and optimize our support efficiency, we use this repository exclusively to track bugs, performance issues, and feature requests.

---

## 🛠️ How to Get Help

### 1. Found a Bug or Have a Request?
Before opening a new issue, please **use the search bar** above to see if someone else has already reported it. 

If it's a new issue:
* Click on the **"Issues"** tab at the top.
* Click **"New Issue"** and select the appropriate template (Bug Report or Feature Request).
* Please fill in as much technical detail as possible (OS version, reproduction steps).

### 2. Join the Discussion
For general chit-chat, tips on multi-format orchestration, or to share your beautiful custom templates, join our active global creator community on Discord:
👉 **[Join our Discord Server](https://discord.gg/Awu7MDGd)**

### 3. Account & Billing Privacy
Because we value your absolute data security, **DO NOT post your email, invoices, or personal data here.** For refund requests or Lemon Squeezy payment inquiries, please email us privately at:
📧 **support@goosewriteapp.com**
